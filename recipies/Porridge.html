Potatoes Porridge

Descriptions

Potatoes porridge is a traditional dish made from potatoes, pepper blend, seasonings, palm oil and water.

It is an easy dish to prepare that is indeginous to the western region of Nigeria

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Pepper blend
  • Seasonings
  • Palm oil
  • Water

Instructions

  1. Peel and dice the potatoes.
  2. Heat the palm oil in a pot and add the diced potatoes.
  3. Add the pepper blend and seasonings.
  4. Stir well and add water.
  5. Simmer until the porridge is cooked through.
