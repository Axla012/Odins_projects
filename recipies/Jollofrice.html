Jollof Rice

Descriptions

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, onions, and a blend of spices.

It is often served with meat, fish, or vegetables and is known for its vibrant red color and rich flavor.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • Bell peppers
  • Garlic
  • Spices (thyme, curry powder, paprika)
  • Meat or fish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pot and sauté onions and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add chopped tomatoes and bell peppers and cook until soft.
  3. Add spices and mix well.
  4. Add rice and stir to coat it with the tomato mixture.
  5. Add water or broth and bring to a boil.
  6. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer until the rice is cooked through.
  7. If using meat or fish, cook them separately and add them to the rice before serving.
