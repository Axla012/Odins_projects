Fried Rice

Descriptions

Fried rice is a popular dish made from cooked rice, vegetables, seasonings, and sometimes meat or seafood.

It is a versatile dish that can be customized with various ingredients and flavors.

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Vegetables (carrots, peas, bell peppers)
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic
  • Eggs (optional)
  • Meat or seafood (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan or wok.
  2. Add garlic and sauté until fragrant.
  3. Add vegetables and cook until tender.
  4. Add cooked rice and stir-fry for a few minutes.
  5. Add soy sauce and mix well.
  6. If using eggs, push the rice to one side of the pan and scramble the eggs on the other side before mixing them together.
  7. If using meat or seafood, cook them separately and add them to the rice before serving.
